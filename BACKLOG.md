# QuitSmoking Feature Backlog

## Planned (to build)

### 1. Craving Journal
- After quit date: log what triggered the craving (stress, coffee, social, boredom, after meal, habit)
- Track intensity (1-5)
- Show patterns over time: "Most cravings happen at 15:00" / "Social situations are your #1 trigger"
- Helps build coping strategies

### 2. Health Timeline
- Show real recovery milestones tied to actual last-smoke time
- Examples: "20 min: heart rate normalizes", "8h: oxygen levels normal", "48h: taste/smell improve", "2 weeks: circulation improves", "1 month: lung function improving", "3 months: cough reduces"
- Visual timeline with checkmarks as you pass each milestone
- Updates live based on time since last cigarette

### 3. Weekly Report Card
- More detailed than current weekly summary notification
- Graphs: daily smoked vs allowance, trend line, skips
- Comparison to previous week
- Highlight achievements: "Best day: Tuesday (0 smoked)", "Longest gap: 14.2h"
- Could be a dedicated tab or a detailed notification with image

## Ideas (parking lot)
